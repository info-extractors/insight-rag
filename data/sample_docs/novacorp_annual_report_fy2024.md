# NovaCorp Technologies Inc. - FY2024 Annual Strategic & Financial Report
**Document ID:** NOVACORP-FY24-10K
**Date of Filing:** October 28, 2024
**Reporting Period:** Fiscal Year Ended September 30, 2024

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Corporate Performance

NovaCorp Technologies Inc. demonstrated resilient financial performance in Fiscal Year 2024 (FY2024), driven primarily by accelerated enterprise adoption of our Cloud Infrastructure and Generative AI platforms. Total consolidated revenue reached **$28.4 billion**, representing an **18.5% year-over-year increase** compared to $24.0 billion in FY2023.

Operating income expanded to **$7.8 billion**, achieving an operating margin of **27.5%**, up 210 basis points from 25.4% in the prior fiscal period. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) grew 22.0% to **$4.88** per share.

The board approved a **$4.0 billion share repurchase authorization** and increased the quarterly cash dividend by 8.0% to **$0.54 per share**.

## 3. Capital Expenditures, R&D, and Cash Flow

Capital expenditures for FY2024 totaled **$4.2 billion**, compared to $3.1 billion in FY2023. Over 75% of capital expenditures were deployed directly toward next-generation GPU compute clusters, high-speed optical networking, and sustainable data center power infrastructure.

Research and Development (R&D) expenses rose to **$5.1 billion**, representing **18.0% of total revenue**. Key R&D investments focused on multimodal foundation models, low-latency reasoning engines, and hardware acceleration compilers.

Free cash flow generated for the fiscal year was **$6.4 billion**, reflecting a cash conversion rate of 82.0% of net income.

## 4. Key Strategic Risks & Operational Headwinds

Management has identified the following principal operational risks for Fiscal Year 2025:
1. **Semiconductor Supply Chain Concentration:** Substantial reliance on specialized high-bandwidth memory (HBM3e) and advanced packaging foundries in Taiwan and South Korea creates vulnerability to geopolitical disruption.
2. **Data Center Energy Constraints:** Grid interconnect backlogs in North America and Western Europe may delay scheduled capacity additions by 6 to 12 months.
3. **Regulatory & Sovereign Compliance:** Emerging AI governance frameworks (including the EU AI Act and cross-border data transfer mandates) require substantial ongoing compliance audits and data residency partitioning.
